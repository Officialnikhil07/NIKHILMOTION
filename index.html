About NikhilMotion

NikhilMotion is a free web radio that plays 90s Hindi film songs around the clock, inspired by the illustrated Indian street-corner barber shop.

The idea is simple: no catalogue to browse, no playlists to build and no complicated setup. Open the page, press play and let the songs keep going.

The rotation includes Hindi playback classics from the late 1980s and 1990s, with singers such as Kumar Sanu, Alka Yagnik, Udit Narayan, Sonu Nigam and others.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NikhilMotion?

NikhilMotion is a free web radio that plays 90s Hindi film songs around the clock, set against an illustrated Indian street-corner barber shop.

Is NikhilMotion free?

Yes. NikhilMotion is free to use and requires no sign-up, subscription, payment or app installation.

How does NikhilMotion work?

Press play in the player. Songs are played through an embedded YouTube player and the next song begins when the current track ends.

What songs does NikhilMotion play?

Hindi film playback songs from the late 1980s and 1990s, including music associated with singers such as Kumar Sanu, Alka Yagnik, Udit Narayan and Sonu Nigam.

Does NikhilMotion play random songs?

Yes. The rotation is shuffled when the page loads, giving each visit a different listening order.

Does NikhilMotion work on mobile?

Yes. The website is designed for phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Is NikhilMotion a music streaming service?

Not in the conventional sense. It is closer to an always-on web radio focused on 90s Hindi film songs.
