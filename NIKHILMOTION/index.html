About

Not a catalogue.
A radio.

NikhilMotion is a free web radio built around 90s Hindi film music. There is no account, subscription, library or playlist to manage. Open the page, press play and let the rotation keep going.

The sound is meant to feel like an old tape playing in a neighbourhood barber shop — familiar, warm and a little imperfect.

Questions

Before the next song.

What is NikhilMotion?

A free web radio that plays 90s Hindi film songs around the clock over an illustrated Indian street-corner barber shop.

Is NikhilMotion free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, subscription, payment or app installation.

How does the player work?

Audio is played through an embedded YouTube player. The controls on this page select and seek through the listening rotation.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The layout is responsive and the same controls work on phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Is it a music streaming service?

It is closer to an always-on web radio than a conventional catalogue-based streaming service.

Rights & contact

Music stays with its owners.

Audio plays through YouTube's embedded player. Nothing is hosted directly on this site. Rights remain with the respective labels, composers and performers. Song credits are assembled from soundtrack listings.
